YOUR DASHBOARD

Adding experiences & managing slots

Everything you need is in one place — your WanderPool dashboard.

Creating an experience listing

  • Title & category — e.g. "Grade IV River Rafting — 16 km" under Water Sports → River Rafting
  • Description — Write what makes your experience unique. Minimum 100 words. Be specific about the route, grade, meeting point.
  • Photos — Minimum 5 real photos (no stock images). Include the activity, equipment, landscape, and your team. First photo becomes the cover.
  • Pricing — Set per-person price in ₹. You can create group-size tiers (e.g. solo ₹1,200 / group of 6+ ₹999/person). Prices must match or be higher than what you charge elsewhere (price parity clause in agreement).
  • Inclusions & exclusions — Be clear. "Helmet, life jacket, guide, transport to put-in point" included. "Meals, accommodation" excluded.
  • Cancellation policy — Choose one of 3 fixed options: Free cancel 48h before / 50% refund 24h before / No refund.
  • Activity details — Our smart form will ask specific questions for your category (e.g. rapids grade, river stretch, min age for rafting; altitude, trek grade, camping for trekking). Fill these accurately — they show on your listing.
  • Itinerary — Optional day-by-day breakdown for multi-day experiences (trekking packages, camping tours).

Managing your availability slots

  • Bulk create recurring slots — Set a date range + time slots + days of week + capacity in one go. E.g. "Every Mon–Sat, 7 AM–10 AM + 11 AM–2 PM, capacity 25, for the next 3 months."
  • Delete a slot — You can delete any slot that has no confirmed bookings. Slots with confirmed bookings are locked.
  • View capacity — Dashboard shows booked_count vs. total capacity per slot in real time. Customers only see slots with remaining capacity.
👥

Adding guides (Agency accounts)

BOOKINGS & EARNINGS

How bookings & payouts work

Once you're live, here's what happens when a customer books.

1

Customer books on WanderPool

Customer picks your experience, selects a date & time slot, pays online via Razorpay (cards, UPI, netbanking). You're notified instantly by email.

2

Customer gets a confirmation + guide intro email

WanderPool automatically sends the booking voucher, meeting point, and an introduction to your guide — all handled by us. You focus on the activity.

3

Mark activity as Started

On the day, open your dashboard and mark the booking as "Started" when the activity begins. This is required to release the payout.

4

Mark activity as Completed

After the activity ends, mark it "Completed." This triggers the payout. Your earnings arrive in your registered bank account within 3 business days.

5

Customer leaves a review

6 hours after activity completion, WanderPool automatically sends a review request to the customer. Good service = better ratings = more bookings. You can reply publicly to any review (300 chars).

Partial payment option

Operators can enable partial payment mode — the customer pays the platform fee + GST online, and the remaining amount in cash at the venue. This reduces online payment hesitation for high-value experiences. Cash must be collected before you can mark the activity as Started.

Tips for a great listing

  • Use real photos — bright, high resolution, showing the actual activity. Listings with 8+ photos get 60% more clicks.
  • Write a specific description. "16 km Grade IV rafting from Shivpuri to Rishikesh" is better than "exciting rafting experience."
  • Be honest about difficulty level, age limits, and fitness requirements. Surprises on the day lead to bad reviews.
  • Respond to customer queries (via WanderPool dashboard) within 2 hours for the best response-time score.
  • Always reply publicly to reviews — positive or negative. It shows professionalism to future customers.
  • Keep your slots updated. Remove unavailable dates promptly. Cancellations hurt your health score.
